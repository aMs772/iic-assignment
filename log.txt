Document: ## Lord Voldemort
Born Tom Marvolo Riddle, Voldemort is the primary antagonist of the series. He is a half-blood, the son of a Muggle father and a witch mother descended from Salazar Slytherin. Obsessed with immortality and blood purity despite his own mixed heritage, he split his soul into seven fragments called Horcruxes to avoid death. His first attempt to kill infant Harry Potter backfired due to the protective magic of Lily Potter's sacrifice, reducing him to a weakened, non-corporeal state for over a decade. He is ultimately defeated in the Battle of Hogwarts when a Killing Curse he casts rebounds on him, since Harry had become the true master of the Elder Wand.

## The Deathly Hallows
Three legendary objects said to make their possessor the "Master of Death": the Elder Wand (the most powerful wand in existence, passed between owners through defeat in combat), the Resurrection Stone (able to summon the shades of the dead, set into a ring originally owned by the Peverell/Gaunt family), and the Cloak of Invisibility (a perfect, never-fading invisibility cloak passed down through the Potter family). Harry Potter ends up in possession of all three by the series' conclusion but chooses not to seek their combined power, instead simply defeating Voldemort and returning the Elder Wand's allegiance through his own death, ensuring no one else would misuse it.

Document: ## Severus Snape
Severus Snape is the Potions Master (and later Defence Against the Dark Arts teacher, then Headmaster) at Hogwarts. Outwardly cold and biased against Harry throughout most of the series, Snape is revealed in the final book to have been working as a double agent for Dumbledore since shortly after Voldemort murdered Lily Potter, the woman Snape loved since childhood. His Patronus, like Lily's, takes the form of a doe, symbolizing his enduring love for her. Snape's loyalty and heroism are only fully understood by Harry after Snape's death via Voldemort's snake, Nagini.
