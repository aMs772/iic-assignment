# What is the Institution's Innovation Council (IIC)?

The Institution's Innovation Council (IIC) is a government-mandated institute body established by the Ministry of Education through the Ministry's Innovation Cell (MIC). Every higher education institution in India is expected to have an IIC to promote innovation, entrepreneurship, intellectual property awareness, and startup culture.

Unlike student clubs, the IIC is an official institute body that reports to the Ministry of Education through the MIC portal. At IIT Bombay, the IIC functions under the Desai Sethi School of Entrepreneurship (DSSE).

Its mission is to strengthen the innovation ecosystem by helping students move from ideas to prototypes, intellectual property, incubation, commercialization, and startups.

====================================================================
WHY DOES IIC EXIST?
====================================================================

The objective of IIC is to ensure that innovation is not limited to isolated student clubs or research groups. Instead, it creates a structured innovation ecosystem within the institute.

The council helps:

- Promote innovation culture
- Encourage entrepreneurship
- Conduct workshops and hackathons
- Facilitate patent filing
- Connect students with mentors
- Support startup incubation
- Report institutional innovation activities to the Government of India

====================================================================
WHY DOES IIC MATTER TO IIT BOMBAY?
====================================================================

IIC serves as the official channel through which IIT Bombay reports innovation activities to the Ministry of Education.

Activities conducted by student clubs, departments, and institute bodies can contribute to IIT Bombay's innovation ranking when reported through IIC.

Participation in IIC helps students gain:

- National recognition
- Patent filing support
- Innovation funding opportunities
- Startup mentorship
- Access to Smart India Hackathon
- Access to YUKTI
- Access to KAPILA
- Innovation Ambassador programs

====================================================================
HOW IS IIC EVALUATED?
====================================================================

The annual score consists of five components.

1. Calendar Activities (40%)

Activities prescribed by MIC including:

- Design Thinking Workshops
- IPR Awareness
- Entrepreneurship Programs
- Field Visits
- Innovation Talks

2. Self Driven Activities (30%)

Institute-specific initiatives such as:

- AI Innovation Chatbot
- Innovation Magazine
- MOOC Courses
- CEO Interview Series
- Innovation Calendar
- BTP/MTP Database

3. MIC Driven Activities (10%)

Activities organized directly by MIC including:

- Innovation Ambassador Program
- India First Leadership Talks
- Regional Meets

4. Research, Commercialization and Idea Cafe (10%)

Activities involving

- Research showcases
- Commercialization
- Idea Cafe
- Innovation discussions

5. Celebration Days (10%)

Examples include

- National Startup Day
- National Science Day
- National Youth Day
- World Intellectual Property Day
